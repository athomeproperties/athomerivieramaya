Your community QR code is sent through your Airbnb messages once the townhouse is cleaned and ready for you — usually right around the 3:00 PM check-in time. No code yet? Just message us on Airbnb.

  1. At the main Mayakoba entrance, tell the guard you're going to Lagunas de Mayakoba and show your ID.

  2. At the Lagunas de Mayakoba gate, scan the QR code we sent you to get in.

  3. Park in one of your two spaces marked V20 and head to your townhouse.

Check-in is any time after 3:00 PM. Check-out is by 10:00 AM.

Heads up: please have a valid photo ID ready for the guard at the main Mayakoba entrance.

06 — Extra Services

Make your stay easier

Pre-arrival grocery stocking

We can have your kitchen stocked before you arrive, subject to availability. The cost is 20% of the grocery total, with a $40 USD minimum charge. Please request at least one week before arrival — just message us on Airbnb with your list.

Mid-stay cleaning

Your reservation fee already covers a cleaning after checkout. If you'd like a cleaning during your stay, it's available for $50 USD per day, subject to availability. Please request at least one week before arrival.

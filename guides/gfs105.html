02 — Parking

Where to park

Street parking

There's no garage at this building — parking is on the street nearby. It's metered from roughly 10:00 AM to 10:00 PM, at about $6 pesos per hour. Outside those hours, street parking is free.

05 — Extra Services

Make your stay easier

Pre-arrival grocery stocking

We can have your kitchen stocked before you arrive, subject to availability. The cost is 20% of the grocery total, with a $35 USD minimum charge. Please request at least one week before arrival — just message us on Airbnb with your list.

Mid-stay cleaning

Your reservation fee already covers a cleaning after checkout. If you'd like a cleaning during your stay, it's available for $40 USD per day, subject to availability. Please request at least one week before arrival.

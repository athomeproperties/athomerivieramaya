03 — The Balcony Jacuzzi

Getting it warm

The tub on your private balcony is jetted, like a jacuzzi bathtub — it's separate from the hot tubs on the rooftop pool deck. It doesn't heat on its own, but here's the trick to get it warm:

  1. Turn on both the hot and cold taps and fill the tub about 3/4 of the way.

  2. Turn both taps off and let it sit for 15–20 minutes.

  3. Turn on only the hot water and fill the rest of the way up.

Good to know: the hot tap closes clockwise like normal — but the cold tap closes counter-clockwise. Easy to miss, so if it's still running, try turning it the other way.
